why ente?

Most apps want to know you better. 😈 We don't. 🥱

Privacy at Ente isn't a lifestyle brand or a moody dark theme. It's a product decision: your files are encrypted on your device, and we never get the keys.

Your data is encrypted before it leaves your device.

Which means we don't have the keys, the contents or a useful excuse to be snooping around in your life.

We don't sell ads.

So your attention isn't the product, and your memories don't have to audition for a recommendation engine.

We don't train AI on your memories.

Your holiday photos don't need a second career, and your private documents deserve even less ambition from us.

Our code is open.

You don't have to take our word for it. You can inspect the implementation, read the audits and keep us honest.

What Ente knows about you

Less than your weather app, less than your shopping app, and dramatically less than any company calling you part of an ecosystem.

Finally, some user data we can actually read. 😎
